# ADR 0008 — Peer comparison from published surveys, never stored customer data

**Status:** accepted (Week 1)

**Context.** Owners want to know "how do I compare?". Comparing with other customers would require storing their answers, which the brief forbids.

**Decision.** Compare against published, dated surveys (data/reference/smb_benchmarks.csv), the owner's own previous baseline file, and the fictional sample businesses. Show sample size, date, publisher and caveats. When surveys disagree (incident-response plans: 69% in Nationwide 2024 vs 34% in Guardz 2025), show both.

**Revisit** only if ChiEAC later wants opt-in, aggregated, anonymized workshop statistics. That would need a privacy review and written consent, and is out of scope for v1.
